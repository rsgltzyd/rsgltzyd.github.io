A notebook is useful because it holds the unfinished version of an idea. A sentence can be awkward. A sketch can be wrong. The page does not mind.

I have been trying to write down observations before turning them into conclusions. What happened, what surprised me, what I still do not understand. The explanation can come later, after the details have had time to settle.

It is a modest practice, but it makes room for better questions.
